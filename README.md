# WoW Imito

**Your Azeroth. Your character. Now on Android.**

## What is WoW Imito?

WoW Imito brings **World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King** to your **Android phone or tablet**, so you can play on the **same compatible WotLK server as your friends on PC**. Complete quests, explore Azeroth and join your friends with **touchscreen or controller controls**. The game runs directly on your device: no PC is needed while you play.

**[Download APK](https://github.com/MrAnonymz/WoW-Imito/releases/latest)** | **[Report a problem](https://github.com/MrAnonymz/WoW-Imito/issues/new/choose)**

## What you need

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Android** | **12 or newer** |
| **Processor** | **ARM64**, with a 64-bit Android system |
| **Recommended CPU performance** | **Snapdragon 888 class or better**; slower chips can run the game at reduced FPS |
| **RAM** | **6 GB suggested minimum**, **8 GB or more recommended** |
| **Free storage for installation** | About **22 GB** for standard WotLK, **30 GB** for tested Frostmourne, **50 GB** for tested CoA; varies with your client |
| **Game client** | Your own complete **WoW 3.3.5a (build 12340)** client |
| **Server account** | An account on a compatible WotLK server and an internet connection |

The APK does not include the game files or a server account. CPU and RAM figures are practical recommendations, not a guarantee of 40–60 FPS.

## Start playing

1. **Install** the APK and open WoW Imito.
2. Tap **Game files** and select your client folder, or paste a direct ZIP download link to your client.
3. Open **Settings > Set realm** and enter your server address.
4. Tap **PLAY**, sign in inside WoW and choose your character.

Movement, camera controls, spells and menus are adapted for touch. **Low specs** helps you reduce graphics settings, and **Eco mode** lets you limit the game to 30 FPS to save battery.

## New in 1.1.0

- Support for the tested **CoA/Descension** and **Whitemane Frostmourne Rebuffed** clients, with automatic mobile compatibility fixes for recognised builds.
- **Xbox, PlayStation, Odin 2 and compatible Android controllers**, with optional **ConsolePort** controls and matching button symbols. DualSense touchpad support is included.
- **LoseControl**, included and **OFF by default**.

### Tested performance

| Client | Test location | Resolution | Measured FPS on Samsung S26 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard WotLK 3.3.5a | Sunstrider Isle | 1280 × 720 | **~60** |
| CoA/Descension | Local test realm | 800 × 450 | **~52** |
| Frostmourne Rebuffed | **Whitemane Frostmourne**, Sunstrider Isle | 800 × 450 | **~45–47** |

These are sample results from different scenes, not guaranteed FPS. Official CoA server login has not been tested. Xbox and DualSense were tested on S26; Odin 2 support has not been tested on physical hardware.

### Text rendering

For **Frostmourne Rebuffed**, open **Settings > Text rendering**. Try **Original WoW** for performance; choose **Rebuffed: safe** or **Rebuffed: full** to use its enhanced text renderer. Restart the game after changing it. The Frostmourne results above used **Original WoW**.

Standard WotLK and CoA/Descension need no change here. **Low specs** and **Defaults** preserve this separate choice.

## Make it your own

These addons are included and **OFF by default**. Turn on the ones you want in **AddOns** on the character-selection screen.

| Addon | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| **Bagnon** | All your bags in one window |
| **ConsolePort** | Controller interface and configurable bindings |
| **LoseControl** | Icons showing crowd-control effects and their duration |
| **NotPlater** | Customisable nameplates |
| **OmniCC** | Cooldown timers on your spells |
| **Titan Panel** | An information bar, including battery percentage and FPS |
| **Immersion** | A new look for quest and NPC conversations |
| **AtlasLoot Enhanced** | Dungeon, raid and other loot tables |
| **GearScoreLite** | Equipment scores |
| **Deadly Boss Mods** | Boss warnings and timers |
| **SexyMap** | Minimap customisation |
| **X-Perl UnitFrames** | Alternative player, target and party frames |

**MoveAnything** and **MinimapButtonBag** are enabled by default to help you arrange the interface and organise minimap buttons. If you change addon activation while playing, type **`/reload`** to apply it.

With a controller connected at game startup, you can accept the in-game prompt to enable **ConsolePort**. Starting without a controller disables ConsolePort.
