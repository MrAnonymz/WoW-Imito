# WoW Imito 1.2.1

27 October 2026

## Changes

- **Update your game directly on Android.** Supported clients are checked when you tap **PLAY**. Choose **Update** when a new update is available, or check manually in **Settings > Update game**.
- Added direct updates for **Project Epoch Reborn** and the **CoA / Descension community client**. **Wrath Rebuffed** checks for updates and uses its built-in game updater.
- **Pause and resume downloads**, including after reopening the app. Downloads can continue in the background.
- Updates preserve your **graphics settings, addons and server address**. Downloaded files are checked before installation, and unfinished updates must be completed before playing.
