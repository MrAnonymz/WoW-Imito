# WoW Imito

**Your Azeroth. Your character. Now on Android.**

Play **World of Warcraft — Vanilla, The Burning Crusade, Wrath of the Lich King and Forever** on your **Android phone, tablet or handheld**, on the **same server as your friends on PC**. Explore, quest and play together with **touchscreen or controller controls** — no PC needed while you play.

**[Download APK](https://github.com/MrAnonymz/WoW-Imito/releases/latest)** · **[Release notes](https://github.com/MrAnonymz/WoW-Imito/releases)** · **[Community reports](https://github.com/MrAnonymz/WoW-Imito/issues)**

**New in 1.5.2:** a **live second-screen HUD** with minimap, chat and quests for dual-screen handhelds, a clearer **Touch UI** with movable weapon controls and item/glyph removal, **Spanish** and localized offline patch notes, optional **Vulkan driver imports** for classic clients, automatic discovery of existing **WoW Forever** installations, plus startup, keyboard, graphics synchronization and cache fixes.

**World of Warcraft - Forever** includes Imito Touch UI and official Battle.net login, downloads and updates. **Forever requires 8 GB RAM or more.** Select Forever in the client list, tap PLAY and sign in to Battle.net. First setup may take a few minutes. **ECO 30-40 FPS is strongly recommended** to reduce CPU/GPU load and temperatures.

## Start playing

**You need Android 12 or newer, an ARM64 processor with 64-bit Android, and your own complete compatible WoW client.**

1. **Install** the APK and open WoW Imito.
2. Copy your client to your phone or SD card, or use **Game files** to download it from a ZIP link.
3. The app **finds your clients automatically**. Choose one and **confirm or change the server address** when prompted.
4. Tap **PLAY**, sign in inside WoW and choose your character.

You can still select a folder in **Game files** or change your server in **Settings > Set realm**.

**App updates are offered automatically.** From version **1.3.3 onward**, supported updates download only the changed parts, even if you skipped releases.

Enable **Options > Support > Automatically update WoW Imito** for automatic downloads and installation from the launcher. Android may request installation permission or confirmation.

Tap **WoW Imito** or the **version number** at the bottom left of the launcher to read the installed version's release notes offline.

**New to touch controls?** Open **Menu > Touch UI > Show tutorial** for the interactive guide.

Choose **Options > Game > Language** for English, German, Czech, Polish, Russian, Italian, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Turkish or Arabic. The app, Imito Touch UI and offline patch notes use your selection; English is the default. Translations were made with AI and may contain mistakes. Please report incorrect wording through the app or a GitHub issue.

In **Touch UI**, move controls or tap one to change its individual size. Choose **Save & reload** to keep your layout. To keep a small chat visible while playing, open **Chat** and turn **Pin** on.

On Android handhelds with **two separate displays**, enable **Options > Controller > Second-screen controls**, connect the second display before starting the game, and choose it when prompted. The lower HUD shows the minimap, chat, watched quests and player/target information. Switch to target controls, trackpad or keyboard as needed, and use **Displays** to reconnect after cancelling or disconnecting.

<details>
<summary><strong>Included addons & controls</strong></summary>

> The app includes addons **compatible with your client version**. Availability differs between Vanilla, TBC and Wrath. These optional addons are **OFF by default**; enable the ones you want in **AddOns** on the character-selection screen.

| Addon | What it adds |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bagnon** | All your bags in one window |
| **ConsolePort** | Controller interface and configurable bindings |
| **LoseControl** | Crowd-control effects and their duration |
| **NotPlater** | Customisable nameplates |
| **OmniCC** | Cooldown timers on your spells |
| **Titan Panel** | Information bar with battery percentage and FPS |
| **Immersion** | A new look for quest and NPC conversations |
| **AtlasLoot Enhanced** | Dungeon, raid and other loot tables |
| **GearScoreLite** | Equipment scores |
| **Deadly Boss Mods** | Boss warnings and timers |
| **SexyMap** | Minimap customisation |
| **X-Perl UnitFrames** | Alternative player, target and party frames |

**MoveAnything** and **MinimapButtonBag** are **ON by default** to help arrange the interface and organise minimap buttons. After changing addon activation while playing, type **`/reload`** to apply it.

On **Wrath**, choose **WoW Imito Touch UI** or **ConsolePort**, and keep **WoW Imito Input** enabled. The two interfaces are mutually exclusive. Connecting or disconnecting a controller does not automatically enable ConsolePort or change your selection.

Original **Vanilla 1.12.1** and **TBC 2.4.3** also offer optional **ConsolePort Legacy** with four action layers, spell/item/macro assignments, cursor controls and keyboard access. Enable it in **AddOns**, then reload. It is disabled by default; disable it and reload to return to Touch UI.

Open **Touch UI** in the in-game menu to resize or move controls and choose the optional **4-way turn joystick**. The original joystick is the default. Use **W/R** to switch between walking and running.

**New to touch controls?** Choose **Show tutorial** when prompted, or start it from **Touch UI**. Follow the highlighted controls to learn movement, spells, items, macros and custom layouts by trying them yourself.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Updating your game</strong></summary>

> For supported clients, **PLAY** checks for updates automatically. Choose **Update** when prompted, or open **Settings** and use **Update** followed by your selected client's name.

- **Project Epoch Reborn** and the **CoA / Descension community client** download updates directly in the app.
- **Wrath Rebuffed**, **Grimfall**, **OctoWoW** and **Triumvirate** update directly in WoW Imito.
- **WoW Forever** uses official Battle.net for verification, downloads and updates.
- Downloads can continue **in the background** and **resume after an interruption**.
- Your **graphics settings, addons and server address** are preserved.

Standard Vanilla, TBC and Wrath clients use your server's usual update method. Their **Update** button in Options is disabled.

</details>
