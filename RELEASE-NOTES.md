# WoW Imito 1.5.11

- Wrath: toggle quest markers on the touch map and choose a learned mount from the spellbook to assign it to an action slot.
- Fixed graphics profile selection for black tiles in Wrath and texture glitches in Forever on affected Mali-G720 drivers.
- Classic clients now process only the texture uploads in the submitted commands and reuse the source mapping across mip levels.
- Forever: if the first Battle.net installation stalls, Reload can fetch a fresh installer and retry without removing game data.
- Touch pages A, B and C show slots 1–11 of the main, bottom-left and bottom-right action bars. Bar 4 opens at the bottom center and bar 5 at the top center. Enable extra bars in the game settings. Drag their numbered handle to move them, or tap it to assign a spell, macro or item. Bar 6 is available on clients that provide it.
- Fixed flicker in the joystick and touch controls after moving or resizing them. If the game sends an incomplete layout, the HUD keeps the last confirmed positions without an extra GPU read.
- On bars 4 and 5, pick food or items straight from your bags. Forever also opens the profession book and mount journal from the slot picker.
- The launcher guide now welcomes new players and shows where to send feedback. The button at the top of the patch notes opens WoW Imito on GitHub.
