# WoW Imito

**Your Azeroth. Your character. Now on Android.**

## What is WoW Imito?

WoW Imito brings **World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King** to your **Android phone or tablet**, so you can play on the **same WotLK server as your friends on PC**. Complete quests, explore Azeroth and join your friends with **touchscreen or controller controls**. The game runs directly on your device: no PC is needed while you play.

## What you need

| Component | Requirement / recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Android** | **12 or newer** |
| **Architecture** | **ARM64 processor + 64-bit Android** |
| **CPU performance** | Suggested starting point: **Snapdragon 888 class or better** |
| **Memory** | **6 GB RAM** suggested minimum · **8 GB+** recommended |
| **Storage — standard WotLK** | About **22 GB free** before installation |
| **Storage — Wrath Rebuffed** | About **30 GB free** before installation |
| **Storage — tested CoA client** | About **50 GB free** before installation |
| **Game files** | Your own complete **WoW 3.3.5a (build 12340)** client |
| **Connection** | Internet access and an account on your chosen compatible server |

The APK does not include game files or a server account. Storage needs depend on your client. CPU and RAM figures are recommendations; slower devices may run the game at reduced FPS.

**Performance varies by device:** CPU, GPU, RAM, graphics settings, resolution, enabled addons, device temperature, battery level and power-saving mode can all affect FPS. Further performance optimizations are in active development.

## Start playing

1. **Install** the APK and open WoW Imito.
2. Tap **Game files** and select your client folder, or paste a direct ZIP download link to your client.
3. Open **Settings > Set realm** and enter your server address.
4. Tap **PLAY**, sign in inside WoW and choose your character.

Movement, camera controls, spells and menus are adapted for touch. **Low specs** helps you reduce graphics settings, and **Eco mode** lets you limit the game to 30 FPS to save battery.

Wrath Rebuffed used **Settings > Text rendering > Original WoW**. CoA / Descension was tested on a local test realm. For Project Epoch Reborn, use a complete client updated with its official launcher.

## Make it your own

These addons are included and **OFF by default**. Turn on the ones you want in **AddOns** on the character-selection screen.

| Addon | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| **Bagnon** | All your bags in one window |
| **ConsolePort** | Controller interface and configurable bindings |
| **LoseControl** | Icons showing crowd-control effects and their duration |
| **NotPlater** | Customisable nameplates |
| **OmniCC** | Cooldown timers on your spells |
| **Titan Panel** | An information bar, including battery percentage and FPS |
| **Immersion** | A new look for quest and NPC conversations |
| **AtlasLoot Enhanced** | Dungeon, raid and other loot tables |
| **GearScoreLite** | Equipment scores |
| **Deadly Boss Mods** | Boss warnings and timers |
| **SexyMap** | Minimap customisation |
| **X-Perl UnitFrames** | Alternative player, target and party frames |

**MoveAnything** and **MinimapButtonBag** are enabled by default to help you arrange the interface and organise minimap buttons. If you change addon activation while playing, type **`/reload`** to apply it.

Choose **WoW Imito Touch UI** or **ConsolePort** in AddOns and keep **WoW Imito Input** enabled. The two interfaces are mutually exclusive. Connecting or disconnecting a controller does not automatically enable ConsolePort or change your choice.

## Help improve WoW Imito

Found something that does not work? Open **Settings > Game crash report** in the app. It includes device and game diagnostics, and you can optionally describe what happened before sending. You can also **[report a problem on GitHub](https://github.com/MrAnonymz/WoW-Imito/issues/new/choose)**. A screenshot or short video helps.
