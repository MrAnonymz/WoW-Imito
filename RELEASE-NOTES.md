# WoW Imito 1.3.6

## Changes

- Added **Second spell row** in the in-game **Touch UI size** menu. It is **enabled by default**.
- Turn it off for the original six spell slots, or back on for eleven. Your extra spell assignments and saved positions are kept.
- Available in **Vanilla, The Burning Crusade and Wrath of the Lich King**. The choice is saved after reloads and restarts.

## Fixes

- Improved handling of graphics-memory failures on the OpenGL path.

The crash reported in **[#44](https://github.com/MrAnonymz/WoW-Imito/issues/44)** is **not yet confirmed resolved** on the affected device. If it happens again, use **Settings → Report problem** after updating.
